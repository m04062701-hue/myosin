Privacy Policy

World Quiz — Geography Game
Effective date: 24 August 2026 · Last updated: 24 August 2026
The short version: World Quiz does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and it has no user accounts. Your scores and settings are stored only on your own device. The only data leaving your device is handled by Apple (maps, Game Center, purchases) and by Google AdMob, which serves the ads that keep the game free. You can turn off personalised ads at any time.

1. Who we are

World Quiz (“the app”, “the game”) is an iOS geography game developed and published by Muneer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the app and its service providers handle when you play, and what choices you have.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

2. Information we do not collect

We want to be explicit about this, because a map game naturally raises the question:

  • We do not collect your geographic location. The app never requests GPS or location permission. The pins you drop on the world map are guesses in a game — they are unrelated to where you actually are, and they are not transmitted to us.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, photos, contacts, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not operate our own servers that store personal data about you, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress locally using Apple’s standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This includes:

  • High scores for each game mode, best streak, most rounds, and daily-challenge results
  • Lifetime XP and rank progress
  • Gameplay statistics (accuracy, average distance, bullseyes, flags and countries solved)
  • Your settings (sound on/off, haptics on/off, reminder preference, tutorials seen)

This information stays on your device. We cannot read it. Deleting the app removes it permanently, and the in-app Reset All Records option erases it at any time.

4. Third-party services

To provide the game we rely on the following services. Each has its own privacy policy, and data handled by them is governed by those policies.

Advertising and your choices

Ads keep World Quiz free. On iOS, apps must ask permission before tracking you across other companies’ apps and websites. If you decline that request — or if you have never been asked — you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised: chosen by general context rather than by an advertising profile.

  • You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • If we offer an ad-free purchase, buying it removes ads from the app entirely.

5. Notifications

The app can send you optional reminders — for example when a new Daily Challenge is available. These are local notifications scheduled on your device; there is no push server and no message content is sent over the internet. We ask for permission only after you have played, and you can revoke it in iOS Settings or turn reminders off inside the app at any time.

6. Children’s privacy

World Quiz is a general-audience geography game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not ask for personal details from any user, regardless of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us and we will assist in having it removed. Parents can also disable advertising personalisation and in-app purchases entirely using Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions on the device.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data — including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict its processing, and the right to object to processing or withdraw consent. This includes users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom (GDPR) and residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), who additionally have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, the most direct way to erase everything the app has is to delete the app or use Reset All Records in its settings. For data held by Google or Apple, please use the rights and controls described in their policies linked above. You may also contact us at any time using the address at the bottom of this page, and we will help where we can.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under California law, beyond the advertising identifier handling described in section 4, which you can disable as explained there.

8. Data retention and security

Game data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Data processed by Apple and Google is retained according to their own schedules. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, the app minimises risk by design: it collects no personal identifiers of its own and maintains no user database.

9. International users

The app is available worldwide. Our service providers (Apple and Google) may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in their respective privacy policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves — for example when a new feature or service provider is added. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version, and material changes will be highlighted here. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

سياسة الخصوصية — World Quiz

باختصار: لعبة World Quiz لا تطلب اسمك ولا بريدك ولا رقم هاتفك ولا موقعك الجغرافي، ولا تحتوي على حسابات مستخدمين. نتائجك وإعداداتك محفوظة على جهازك وحده. البيانات الوحيدة التي تغادر الجهاز تتولاها Apple (الخرائط، Game Center، المشتريات) وGoogle AdMob التي تعرض الإعلانات التي تُبقي اللعبة مجانية، ويمكنك إيقاف الإعلانات المخصّصة متى شئت.

ما لا نجمعه

  • لا نجمع موقعك الجغرافي إطلاقاً — التطبيق لا يطلب إذن الموقع، والدبابيس التي تضعها على الخريطة مجرد تخمينات داخل اللعبة ولا علاقة لها بمكانك الحقيقي.
  • لا نطلب إنشاء حساب، ولا نجمع الاسم أو البريد أو الهاتف أو الصور أو جهات الاتصال.
  • لا نملك خوادم تخزّن بيانات شخصية عنك، ولا نبيع أي معلومات لأي جهة.

ما يُحفظ على جهازك

تُحفظ أرقامك القياسية، ونقاط الخبرة والرتبة، والإحصائيات (الدقة ومتوسط المسافة والأعلام والدول)، وإعداداتك (الصوت والاهتزاز والتذكيرات) داخل جهازك فقط عبر التخزين المحلي من Apple. لا يمكننا الاطلاع عليها، وحذف التطبيق أو استخدام خيار «إعادة تعيين الأرقام» يمسحها نهائياً.

خدمات الطرف الثالث

  • Google AdMob — لعرض الإعلانات، وقد تعالج معرّف الإعلانات وعنوان IP ونوع الجهاز ونظام التشغيل لعرض الإعلانات وقياسها.
  • Apple Game Center — اختياري، وعند تسجيل الدخول تُرفع نتائجك واسمك المستعار إلى لوحات الصدارة، وتتحكم Apple بهذه البيانات.
  • Apple MapKit — لتحميل صور الخريطة، ويتطلب اتصالاً بالإنترنت.
  • مشتريات داخل التطبيق — تتولى Apple عملية الدفع بالكامل، ولا نرى بيانات بطاقتك أو حسابك إطلاقاً.

خياراتك بشأن الإعلانات

يمكنك رفض التتبّع من الإعدادات ← الخصوصية والأمان ← التتبّع، وحينها تستمر الإعلانات لكن تصبح غير مخصّصة. كما يمكنك تصفير معرّف الإعلانات من الخصوصية والأمان ← إعلانات Apple، أو شراء خيار إزالة الإعلانات إن توفّر.

الإشعارات

التذكيرات محلية تُجدول على جهازك فقط (لا يوجد خادم إشعارات)، ونطلب الإذن بعد أن تلعب لا قبله، ويمكنك إيقافها من داخل التطبيق أو من إعدادات النظام في أي وقت.

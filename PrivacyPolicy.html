Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

PerfectStop (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting the privacy of our players. This Privacy Policy details how data is collected, handled, and used when you interact with our mobile application.

By downloading, installing, or using PerfectStop, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We value your privacy above all. PerfectStop is designed to be fully playable with no account registration, login requirements, or profile creation. Because of this:

  • We do not collect, request, or store your name, phone number, address, or personal contact details.
  • We do not operate any database servers to collect or save user profiles.

2. Third-Party Service Providers

To support the development of our free-to-play app, we utilize standard, reputable third-party tools which collect anonymized operational data:

A. Advertising and Analytics (Google AdMob)

We integrate the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve standard banner and interstitial ads. Google AdMob may automatically collect and use device advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), coarse geographic location (derived from IP address), and usage statistics (ad interaction, taps, views, and app diagnostics) to deliver relevant ads and compile anonymous performance reports.

Learn more: Google Partner Terms & Privacy Policy

B. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit)

Purchases of our premium upgrades (such as removing ads) are processed directly and securely by Apple's systems. The developer has absolutely no access to, nor stores, your payment credentials, credit card numbers, or bank accounts. Apple provides us with anonymized tokens to locally verify and unlock your premium items.

3. Your Controls and Tracking Opt-Out

You maintain full control over your privacy settings within PerfectStop:

  • App Tracking Transparency (ATT): Upon first launch on iOS, you can decline permission to track, ensuring that AdMob only serves non-personalized ads without accessing your IDFA.
  • Reset Advertising ID: You can reset or limit ad tracking at any time by going to your iOS device settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).

4. Children's Privacy

PerfectStop does not intentionally or knowingly target or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any third-party SDK has collected children's data inadvertently, please contact us immediately to address the issue.

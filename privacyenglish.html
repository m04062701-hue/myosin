Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026

Overview

EngLevel ("the App") is an English proficiency testing app. We designed it to collect as little data as possible. The App has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

Information stored on your device

The following data is created and stored only on your device. It is never uploaded to us and we have no access to it:

  • Your test answers and results (CEFR levels, skill scores, and test history)
  • Your app preferences (such as whether you have seen the welcome screens)

Deleting the App, or using "Clear test history" in Settings, permanently removes this data.

Writing evaluation

If you choose to complete the optional writing section, the text you write is sent securely (over HTTPS) to Google's Gemini API for automated evaluation. This is the only data that ever leaves your device.

  • The text is sent for the sole purpose of generating your writing assessment.
  • No name, email, device identifier, or other personal information is sent with it.
  • Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • You can always skip the writing section — the rest of the test works entirely offline.

We recommend that you do not include personal information (names, addresses, phone numbers) in your writing answers.

Subscriptions and payments

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through your App Store account. We never see or store your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in the Apple Privacy Policy.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect, sell, rent, or share personal data.
  • We do not use advertising or advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or profiling.
  • We do not knowingly collect any data from children. The App stores results locally regardless of the user's age.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes (such as introducing accounts or analytics) will be clearly communicated inside the App.

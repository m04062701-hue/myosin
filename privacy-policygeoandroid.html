Privacy Policy

World Quiz — Geography Game
Effective date: 15 September 2026 · Last updated: 15 September 2026
The short version: World Quiz does not ask for your name, email, phone number or location, and it has no user accounts. Your scores and settings are stored only on your own device. The only data leaving your device is handled by Google — maps, ads, and (if you use them) Play Games leaderboards and Play purchases. You can turn off personalised ads at any time, and in regions where consent is required you choose before any ad is requested.

1. Who we are

World Quiz (“the app”, “the game”) is an Android geography game developed and published by Muneer (“we”, “us”), and distributed through Google Play. This policy explains what information the app and its service providers handle when you play, and what choices you have.

If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

2. Information we do not collect

We want to be explicit about this, because a map game naturally raises the question:

  • We do not collect your geographic location. The app declares no location permission and never requests one. The pins you drop on the world map are guesses in a game — they are unrelated to where you actually are, and they are not transmitted to us.
  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, photos, contacts, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not operate our own servers that store personal data about you, and we do not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

The only permission the app requests at install time is internet access, which is needed to load map imagery and ads. On Android 13 and newer it also asks for permission to post notifications, and only after you have already played a game.

3. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress locally using Android’s standard on-device storage (SharedPreferences). This includes:

  • High scores for each game mode, best streak, most rounds, and daily-challenge results
  • Your daily-challenge streak and the date you last played it
  • Lifetime XP and rank progress
  • Gameplay statistics (accuracy, average distance, bullseyes, flags and countries solved)
  • Your settings (sound on/off, haptics on/off, reminder preference, tutorials seen)

This information stays on your device. We cannot read it. Uninstalling the app removes it, and the in-app Reset All Records option erases it at any time.

Android Backup: if you have Google’s automatic backup enabled for your device, Android may include this local game data in your encrypted Google account backup so your progress survives a new phone. That backup is controlled entirely by you in Settings → Google → Backup, and we have no access to it.

Advertising and your choices

Ads keep World Quiz free. The game shows a banner on the main menu, a full-screen ad after a run has ended and you tap to continue, an ad when you return to the app after being away, and optional rewarded videos you may choose to watch in exchange for an in-game bonus such as an extra heart or a hint. Rewarded videos are always your choice — declining one never costs you anything.

  • If you are in the EEA, the UK or another region with consent requirements, the app shows Google’s certified consent form before any ad is requested. If you decline, no personalised ads are requested. You can reopen that form at any time from Settings → Privacy options inside the app.
  • You can opt out of ad personalisation for every app on your phone in Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation, or remove the identifier entirely with Delete advertising ID. You will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised: chosen by general context rather than by an advertising profile.
  • If we offer an ad-free purchase, buying it removes ads from the app entirely.

5. Notifications

The app can send you optional reminders — for example when a new Daily Challenge is available. These are local notifications scheduled on your device; there is no push server and no message content is sent over the internet. On Android 13 and newer we ask for notification permission only after you have played, and you can revoke it in Settings → Apps → World Quiz → Notifications or turn reminders off inside the app at any time.

6. Children’s privacy

World Quiz is a general-audience geography game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). We do not ask for personal details from any user, regardless of age.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through a third-party service used by the app, please contact us and we will assist in having it removed. Parents can also supervise the device, restrict purchases and limit ad personalisation using Google Family Link and the parental controls in the Google Play Store.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal data — including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict its processing, and the right to object to processing or withdraw consent. This includes users in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom (GDPR) and residents of California (CCPA/CPRA), who additionally have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, the most direct way to erase everything the app has is to uninstall it or use Reset All Records in its settings. Consent for advertising can be withdrawn at any time through Settings → Privacy options in the app. For data held by Google, please use the rights and controls described in its policies linked above. You may also contact us at any time using the address at the bottom of this page, and we will help where we can.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under California law, beyond the advertising identifier handling described in section 4, which you can disable as explained there.

8. Data retention and security

Game data lives on your device for as long as the app is installed. Data processed by Google is retained according to its own schedules. All network traffic between the app and Google’s services is encrypted in transit. While no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, the app minimises risk by design: it collects no personal identifiers of its own and maintains no user database.

9. International users

The app is available worldwide. Our service provider (Google) may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in its privacy policy.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves — for example when a new feature or service provider is added. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page always reflects the current version, and material changes will be highlighted here. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

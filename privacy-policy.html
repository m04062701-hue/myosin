Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 11, 2026

TokSave is designed to help you save and organize supported video links. This policy explains what information is handled when you use the app.

Information stored on your device

TokSave stores your library locally on your device. This may include saved links, titles, creator names, thumbnails, categories, notes, tags, reminders, favorites, and successful activity records. TokSave does not require an account.

Link processing

When you ask TokSave to process a supported link, that link may be sent to the linked website and a third-party processing service to retrieve information needed for the requested feature. These services receive technical information normally included in an internet request, such as an IP address and device networking information.

Photo Library

If you choose to save supported media to Photos, TokSave requests add-only access. This permission allows the app to add the selected item but does not allow it to browse or read your existing photo library.

Advertising

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from IP, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver, measure, and protect advertising. Availability and use of personalized advertising depend on your consent and regional requirements. See Google’s Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. TokSave receives subscription entitlement information but does not receive or store your payment card details. See Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Remote configuration

TokSave may retrieve a small configuration file hosted on GitHub to determine whether certain app features are available. This request may provide GitHub with standard network information. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, notification scheduling is handled on your device. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored information remains until you delete it in TokSave or remove the app. You can delete individual library items and categories from within the app. Information handled independently by Apple, Google, GitHub, or linked services is subject to their own retention policies.

Children

TokSave is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards and minimize the information handled by TokSave. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices

  • Manage Photos and notification permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Choose whether to use optional link-processing features.
  • Manage advertising privacy choices when presented.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • Delete locally stored content in the app or by removing TokSave.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when TokSave or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version.
